Some claim that Korean boy band, BTS, have "westernized." In other words, their music has, over time, included more and more english lyics. Here we can see that this does appear to be true, with a general pattern of increased Enlgligh lyrics over time. However, this does not account for trends in the wider industry. Is it just BTS, or has K-Pop as a whole started on a path towards appealing to a more global audience through their lyrics.

Through a recent survey, fans of BTS on twitter reported how they perceieve BTS in regards to the rest of the K-Pop industry. The results were nearly evenly split between those who exclusively are fans of BTS and those who are fans of other K-Pop groups in addition to BTS. This led to some interesting differences in perception of BTS' relationship with K-Pop. An overwhelming amount of BTS exclusive fans do not even consider BTS to be considered K-Pop. These results give us a breif look into the fractured relationship between BTS fans and the larger K-Pop community.

This portfolio utilizes a grey, black and red color scheme. This was chosen to match the aesthetics of the photos as well as keep the general look simple and clean. The fonts were for the most part kept as Courier New to maintain a cohesive look, with the exception of Georgia added to the paragraphs for legibility. The graphs maintained the color scheme while using color to convey the important information. Nearly all elements were aligned to the center, except for the paragraphs as I believe it looks neater and more like a proper website.
